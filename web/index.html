Movers this week

Change in lift since the previous weekly run. A tag climbing from 0.95 to 1.20 is a better bet than one flat at 1.15 — the second is already crowded. Tags need at least 5 examples in both weeks to appear.

What the winners have in common

Median score of reels carrying each tag, against the corpus median. Above 1.00 out-performs the field. Measured across every scored reel, not just the top 1000 — comparing winners only to other winners flattens the contrast. Tags with fewer than 5 examples are hidden; they are noise.

How the score works

        

        
Each signal is converted to a percentile rank across the whole corpus before weighting, so no unit dominates. A recency factor then discounts old reels — the point is to learn what works now.
